Bushveld golf on the doorstep of Kruger

Nine holes and eighteen tees, 2 km from Malelane Gate, with a clubhouse, pool and campsite under the trees.

Book a tee time See the course

Malelane Golf Club is a country course in the old Lowveld way: big shade trees, fair green fees, birdsong on every tee and a stoep where the round ends with something cold.

Holes
9 holes, 18 tees
Par
72 over 18 holes
Kruger
2 km to Malelane Gate

A course split by the Kruger road

The R570 to Malelane Gate runs straight through the property. Three holes and the clubhouse sit on one side of the road. The other six are tucked away in the bush on the other side, and on most days you will have them to yourself.

Magnificent old trees line the fairways, a stream winds between the holes, the Lowveld rough punishes a loose drive, and the round closes with a short par 4 back towards the clubhouse. Bring binoculars: the surrounding bush is full of birds.

More than just a game.

Golf, friendship and the Lowveld lifestyle.

Stay for the afternoon, or the weekend

Clubhouse and kitchen

Breakfast before you tee off, a meal at the halfway, and a cold drink on the stoep as the sun goes down. Platters are available to order for functions and golf days.

The pool

A large pool for the hottest Lowveld afternoons.

Camping under the trees

Pitch a tent or park the caravan on the lawn beside the course, a few minutes from the Kruger gate. Stay a night, a week or the whole winter.

See camping rates

Cart hire

Fifteen carts for nine or eighteen holes. On Thursdays, two players and a cart cost R600.

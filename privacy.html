Privacy policy

Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA). Last updated November 2026.

The Malelane Golf Club ("the club", "we", "us") runs a nine-hole golf course, clubhouse and campsite on the R570 in Malelane, Mpumalanga. This policy explains what personal information we collect when you book a round, a campsite or a golf day, or contact us, why we need it, and the rights you have under POPIA.

1. Who is responsible

The responsible party is The Malelane Golf Club, R570, Malelane, 1320. Questions about your personal information go to the club office, which acts as our Information Officer: 013 790 0283, WhatsApp 060 485 7945 or admin@malgc.co.za.

2. What we collect

  • Your name, phone or WhatsApp number and email address.
  • Booking details: the date and time you want to play or camp, the number of players, your handicap or club membership if you share it, and any cart or campsite you book.
  • For members: membership and subscription records.
  • Records of payments, invoices and competition results.

This website does not take payments and does not ask for card or banking details.

3. How the booking form works

The booking form on this website does not store anything on a server. When you press "Send on WhatsApp", your browser opens WhatsApp with your message filled in, and nothing is sent until you send it yourself. Messages you send us on WhatsApp are also handled under WhatsApp's own privacy terms.

4. Why we use it

  • To confirm tee times, campsite bookings and golf day entries, and to contact you about them.
  • To run competitions and keep members informed about club matters.
  • To keep the accounting and tax records the law requires.

We only use your information for these purposes. We do not sell or rent it, and we will not send you marketing messages without your consent.

5. Who we share it with

We share your information only when we need to: with staff and committee members who handle your booking or membership, with the organiser of a golf day you enter, with service providers we rely on to run the club (such as our phone, email and accounting providers), with handicap and golf union systems if you are a member, or when the law requires it.

6. How long we keep it

Booking enquiries are deleted within 12 months. Membership, invoice and payment records are kept for as long as tax law requires (currently five years), then deleted.

7. Keeping it safe

We take reasonable steps to protect your information against loss, misuse and unauthorised access, including password-protected devices and accounts.

8. This website

This website does not use advertising cookies or tracking analytics, and it does not ask you to log in. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep basic technical logs (such as IP addresses) to keep the service secure. The map is provided by Google Maps, which may set its own cookies when it loads. Links to WhatsApp, Facebook, Google Maps and email open services that have their own privacy policies.

9. Your rights

Under POPIA you may ask what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, object to how we use it, and withdraw consent you have given. Contact the club office using the details above. If you are not happy with our response you may complain to the Information Regulator (South Africa) at inforegulator.org.za.

10. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page.
